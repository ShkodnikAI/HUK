How charts work

Charts are arithmetic, not opinion. The same listener actions always produce the same ranking, and AI never moves a track up or down.

What counts

A like counts as +1, a dislike as -1, adding a track to a playlist as +1.5. Listening to at least 80% of a track counts +0.5, once per day per listener, and the same again on another day. Skipping a playlist track early counts -0.5. Dislikes are private: nobody sees them, including the artist.

Newer matters more

Every action loses half of its weight every 7 days, so charts follow what people enjoy now.

Few votes cannot win

We rank by a cautious estimate of how much people like a track (the lower bound of a 95% confidence interval), not by the raw share of likes. Five likes out of five rank below 199 out of 200. A track needs 10 different listeners to enter a chart.

Fair play

Bursts of votes and suspicious patterns are flagged and count as zero. Reactions only unlock after a listener has actually listened for 30 seconds.

Fresh tracks get a chance

A small share of airtime goes to new tracks so they can be heard before they have votes. [Exact share: from the scheduler settings.]
