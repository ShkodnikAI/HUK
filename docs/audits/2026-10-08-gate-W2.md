| # | Sev | Where | Finding | Fix |

| G1 | Medium | `src/worker/index.ts` | The moderation pass and the maintenance jobs start in **every** worker process; only the scheduler is leader-gated. During a rolling restart two workers run `runModerationPass`: the same PENDING track can be moderated twice (double paid calls, duplicate `ModerationRun` rows, racing status updates). `moderateTrack` has no per-track lock. | H-211 |
| G2 | Medium | `src/server/sources/verify.ts`, `src/server/tracks/takedown.ts` | Only `takedownTrack` ends the live slot and deletes future slots. A source **hash mismatch** (suspended) or a source that becomes unavailable leaves the live slot airing and the future slots in the table; `/now` hides them, the scheduler counts the time as covered, so listeners get dead air for up to 30 minutes (reproduced in the simulation: orphan future slots of non-eligible tracks remain). | H-212 |
| G3 | Medium | `src/components/player/radio-player.tsx`, `src/lib/sync/clock.ts` | The player has no audio `error`/`stalled` handling (a dead author host or a removed file leaves it silent for the rest of the slot) and re-polls `/now` only every 30 s, so a takedown reaches a listener who is already playing after up to 30 s. When `current` is null every client polls once per second. | H-212 |
